    Where is my data?

    Entries and your target are stored with the browser's localStorage, grouped by calendar date. Clearing site data or using another browser or device starts a fresh log.

    ⚠️ Calorie values are estimates, and the daily target is not personalised medical or dietary advice. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian for guidance that fits you.
